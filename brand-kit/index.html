Ready for the website

Visual rules

  • Use the navy wordmark with the four-color seasonal emblem on a white or very light background.
  • Use the primary PNG at 280px wide or larger where the service descriptor needs to be readable. Use the companion four-season mark for small icons.
  • Use white text on navy for primary buttons. Use deep ink on bright season colors; reserve white seasonal symbols for decorative graphics.
  • Use 12–20px corner radii and an 8px spacing scale. Keep controls at least 44px tall, with visible focus states.

Content rules

  • Lead with “IU student owned” or “Lawn & exterior care by IU students.” Keep Monroe's own logo and identity.
  • Show all four seasons; let the current season lead the main service offer.
  • List leaf cleanup, mowing, dethatching, gutters, washing, windows, snow, tree and bush lights, debris pickup, and rental exterior cleanup as applicable.
  • Keep ladder work to single-story homes and lights to accessible trees and bushes. Confirm job timing and scope during quoting.
  • Use real customer reviews and verified business claims when available.
