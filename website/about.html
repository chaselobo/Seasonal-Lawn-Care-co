Our story

Hey, we're the students behind Monroe Seasonal Co.

We're IU students helping our neighbors around Bloomington and Monroe County tackle lawn care, cleanups, and the outdoor jobs that pile up with the seasons.

We started Monroe Seasonal Co. while studying at Indiana University, bringing our hands-on experience to lawn and exterior care around Bloomington. We work together, keep things personal, and take pride in the details.
