# Monroe Seasonal Co. — Image Asset List & AI Prompts

Every image slot on the website, with a ready-to-use AI image prompt.

**How to use:** generate (or shoot) each image, save it with the exact filename into `website/images/photos/`, and it appears on the site automatically. No code edits needed.

**Style rules for every image** (from the brand kit): natural light, real-looking work, normal work clothes (navy crew shirts), honest results. No text, logos or watermarks inside images.

> ⚠️ **Real photos matter.** Slots marked **REAL PHOTO** show your actual crew, owners or job results. Use the AI prompt only as a stand-in while designing, and swap in real photos before launch. Showing AI-made people as your team or AI before/afters as your work misleads customers.

### Owner headshot tips
Same spot, same time of day, same navy shirts, phone in portrait mode, shoulders-up, 4:5 vertical. Consistent photos make the four of you look like a real team.
