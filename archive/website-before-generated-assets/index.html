Questions

Good to know.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. Send us a few photos and a short description through the estimate form, and we'll reply with a written quote. Bigger jobs may need a quick in-person look, which is free too.

Do I need to be home while you work?

Nope. Most of our customers aren't. We'll confirm the arrival window, text when we're on the way, and send before-and-after photos when we're finished.

What happens when school is on break?

Describe your plan here, e.g. "At least two owners stay in Bloomington over every break, so recurring mowing and snow customers never miss a visit." This is the #1 question people will have about a student business, so answer it clearly.

Are you insured?

State your insurance coverage plainly here. If you're not insured yet, getting general liability coverage is worth it, and customers (and landlords) ask.

What if it rains or snows on my scheduled day?

We watch the forecast closely. If weather moves your appointment, we'll reach out before your window with the next available time. For snow removal, we head out once accumulation hits your chosen trigger depth.

Do you do two-story gutters or roof work?

For safety, we keep ladder work to single-story homes, and holiday lights to trees, bushes and spots we can reach from the ground or a short ladder. If you need more, we're happy to point you to someone who does.

How do I pay?

List payment methods, e.g. Venmo, Zelle, check, or card via invoice. Say when payment is due.
