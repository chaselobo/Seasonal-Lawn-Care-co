06 / Keep it consistent

Implementation notes

Color & layout

  • Use cream as the main canvas, forest for buttons and large brand areas, ink for body text, and sage for secondary surfaces.
  • Keep gold decorative. Small gold text on cream and white text on gold do not provide sufficient contrast.
  • Space elements in 8px steps: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Limit body paragraphs to about 60 characters per line.
  • Keep interactive controls at least 44px tall and preserve visible keyboard focus.

Logo & content

  • Use full horizontal signatures at 280px or wider and stacked signatures at 240px or wider. Use the compact logo in website headers and the simplified M for favicons.
  • Leave clear space equal to the emblem's sun-to-M gap. Never stretch the mark or recolor individual letters.
  • State IU student ownership in text. Use Monroe's own identity throughout.
  • Add reviews, job photos, insurance statements, and experience claims only when verified. Keep gutter and ladder service scope to single-story homes; holiday lighting to accessible trees and bushes.
